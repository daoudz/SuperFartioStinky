# 💨 SUPER FARTIO BROS. 💩
### *World 1: The Sewer Kingdom*

An 8-bit retro side-scrolling platformer inspired by the classic arcade era, featuring **Fartio**—the gas-powered subterranean plumber on a quest through the toxic pipes of the underworld.

🎮 **Play Online**: [https://daoudz.github.io/SuperFartioStinky/](https://daoudz.github.io/SuperFartioStinky/)

📱 **Mobile Ready**: Fully optimized for **Android** & **iOS** devices with responsive on-screen virtual controls, automatic landscape orientation prompt, haptic vibration feedback, multi-touch support, and full-screen mode!

---

## 🎬 Level Briefing & One-Tap Fullscreen
Before every level, a **briefing splash screen** shows the world number, level name, the **🎯 level goal**, and a quick controls cheat-sheet. The game stays paused (and silent) until you start.

- **⛶ PLAY FULLSCREEN** — enters fullscreen *and* starts the level in a single tap (on Android it also locks the screen to landscape). Perfect for phones, where the browser address bar can hide the top of the page in landscape.
- **▶ PLAY** — starts the level without fullscreen. Desktop players can just press `Enter` or `Space`.
- A floating **⛶** button inside the game screen (just below the HUD) lets touch players toggle fullscreen at any time without hunting for the header button.
- iPhone Safari doesn't allow true fullscreen for web pages, so the game uses an edge-to-edge fallback and suggests **Share → Add to Home Screen** for the full experience.

## 📖 Story & Lore
Deep beneath the city, deep in the subterranean drainage catacombs, lies **The Sewer Kingdom**. The nefarious **King Poopa** and his army of sentient fecal turds, mutating bacteria germs, and winged sewer flies have clogged the Great Drain and seized the Royal Porcelain Throne!

Enter **Fartio**: plumber, chili enthusiast, and master of acoustic propulsion. Armed with bean-powered flatulence, toxic gas balls, and an unstoppable double-fart jump, Fartio must navigate 4 perilous sewer sectors to defeat King Poopa and flush the underworld clean once and for all!

## 🌟 Game Mechanics & Physics

- **Acoustic Fart Jump**: Jumping propels Fartio upward with a burst of synthesized bass flatulence and rising green stink particles. Variable jump control lets you do short hops or hold the key to reach up to **154px** (~4.8 tiles) to easily scale high pipes and bricks.
- **Mid-Air Double Fart**: Tap jump again while airborne to trigger an emergency secondary toot boost that propels you further across lethal gaps!
- **Gas Ball Shooting**: Press `X` / `Shift` / `J` (or the on-screen `☣️ GAS` button) to fire bouncing toxic gas spheres that ricochet off floors and walls, flipping enemies on contact.
- **Block Bumping**: Hit `?` blocks and bricks from underneath to reveal items or launch unsuspecting enemies walking on top of them!
- **Brick Breaking**: Head-bump crusty sewer bricks to crack and smash them into flying debris.

## 👾 Stinky Bestiary

- **Turdion (Poop Enemy)**: Swirled Goomba-like feces with corn specs, angry furrowed brows, and two animated sewer flies buzzing around its head. Stomp from above for a wet squish splat, or vaporize with a gas shot.
- **Germoid (Bacteria Enemy)**: Spiky radioactive green microbe crawling with wriggling cilia and bloodshot eyes. Periodically performs surprise slimy micro-hops.
- **Winged Mutant Sewer Fly**: Aerial pests that swoop along sinusoidal flight paths over sewer chasms. Stomp them while airborne or shoot them down before they cross your bridge!
- **King Poopa (Citadel Boss)**: The 6-HP monarch of the porcelain throne. Wears a golden crown, stomps the castle floor, and breathes toxic green fireball waves. Stomp his head 3 times or barrage him with gas projectiles to knock him into the boiling acid moat!

## 🗺️ World 1 Level Walkthrough

### 🚰 World 1-1: The Great Sewer
*Theme: Sludge Canals & Stink Pipes*
The opening training grounds. Learn the ropes of fart jumping, bump the classic question block clusters, discover your first Baked Beans Can, and sprint across pipe tops to reach the Golden Toilet Flagpole!

### ⚙️ World 1-2: Toxic Pipeworks
*Theme: High Ducts & Grate Lifts*
An industrial vertical pipeline maze. Ride moving elevator platforms across deep bubbling acid vats, dodge swooping mutant flies, and explore overhead ducts stuffed with golden coins.

### 🌊 World 1-3: Septic Lagoon
*Theme: High Catwalk Bridges*
An athletic platformer over a vast bottomless septic lagoon. Precise timing is essential as you leap across oscillating wooden rafts, dodge flying insects, and maintain your footing on narrow suspended bridges.

### 👑 World 1-4: King Poopa's Citadel
*Theme: Royal Porcelain Boss Fortress*
The castle climax! Navigate fiery corridors featuring rotating toxic firebars and boiling sludge moats. Reach the end of the dungeon to confront **King Poopa** in a climactic multi-phase boss fight to reclaim the Golden Throne!

## 🛠️ Technical Architecture

- **Mobile First & Fullscreen**: Responsive touch gamepad designed for Android and iOS touchscreens with multi-touch tracking, haptic vibration pulses, and viewport-fill fullscreen mode.
- **100% Procedural & Hand-Drawn**: Zero external image or audio files! Every sprite, tile, particle, and animation frame is drawn using custom pixel matrices and offscreen canvas routines in [`sprites.js`](sprites.js).
- **Procedural Web Audio API Synthesizer**: Sound effects (wet/bassy fart jumps, squeaky gas shots, squishy splats, fanfare, brick smashes) and all 3 chiptune BGM tracks are procedurally synthesized in [`audio.js`](audio.js) via oscillators, noise buffers, and resonant lowpass/bandpass filters. Audio context automatically unlocks on first touch.
- **Vanilla Modern JavaScript**: No external game engines, build tools, or npm dependencies needed. Runs natively in any modern desktop or mobile browser.
- **Retro CRT Shader**: Optional retro scanline and vignette overlay toggled with a single click.
